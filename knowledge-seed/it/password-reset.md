# Password Reset

## Reset your password yourself

You can reset your company password at any time through the self-service password portal:

1. Go to the password portal (https://password.example.com) from any device.
2. Enter your work email address and complete multi-factor authentication.
3. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters, not used in the last 10 passwords.

After resetting, sign out and back in on your laptop and update the password on your phone's email app.

## Locked account

After 10 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## Lost multi-factor authentication device

If you have lost the phone you use for multi-factor authentication, call the IT Service Desk. They verify your identity before registering a new device.
