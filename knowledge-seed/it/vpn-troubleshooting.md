# VPN Troubleshooting

The company VPN (GlobalConnect client) is required to reach internal systems from outside the office.

## VPN error 809

VPN error 809 means the connection to the VPN server was blocked, usually by a firewall or a hotel or guest Wi-Fi network.

1. Switch to a different network, for example your phone's hotspot, and try again.
2. Make sure the GlobalConnect client is version 6.2 or later (Software Center > GlobalConnect > Update).
3. If the error continues on a home network, restart your router.
4. If none of these steps help, raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk and include the time of the error.

## VPN connects but internal sites do not load

Disconnect and reconnect the VPN. If internal sites still do not load, run **Flush DNS** from the IT Self-Service app, then restart your browser.

## Slow VPN connection

The VPN is slower on video calls. Join Teams calls without the VPN: Teams does not need it.
