# Laptop Requests

## New starters

Line managers request laptops for new starters in the IT Self-Service app at least 10 working days before the start date. Standard laptops are delivered to the office on the first day.

## Replacement laptops

Laptops are replaced every four years. If your laptop is damaged or too slow for your role before then, raise a hardware request in the IT Self-Service app with a description of the problem. Accidental damage is repaired or replaced free of charge once per replacement cycle.

## Contractors

Contractors receive a company laptop only when their contract is longer than three months. Shorter engagements use virtual desktops.
