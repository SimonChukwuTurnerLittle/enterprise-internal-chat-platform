# Salary Bands

Confidential guidance for people managers and HR. Do not share band ranges with employees outside the formal pay review process.

## Band structure

Salary band C ranges from 45,000 to 60,000 per year. Band D ranges from 60,000 to 80,000 per year. Band E ranges from 80,000 to 105,000 per year.

## Pay review

The annual pay review takes place in March. Managers submit pay recommendations in the HR Portal by 15 February. Increases above 8% need approval from the department director and HR.

## New hires

Offers should normally be made in the lower half of the band. Offers above the band midpoint need HR approval.
