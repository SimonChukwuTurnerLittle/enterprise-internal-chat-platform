# Annual Leave Policy

This policy explains how much annual leave Example Corp employees receive and how to book it.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours. For example, an employee working three days a week receives 15 days of annual leave per year.

After five years of continuous service, entitlement increases by one day per year, up to a maximum of 30 days.

## Booking leave

Request annual leave in the HR Portal under **My Leave > New request** at least two weeks in advance for periods longer than three days. Your line manager approves or declines the request within five working days.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days of annual leave can be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they are lost. Exceptions, for example after long-term sickness, need HR approval.

## Leaving the company

Untaken leave is paid in the final salary. Leave taken above the pro-rata entitlement is deducted from the final salary.
