# Sickness Absence Policy

## Reporting sickness

If you are too unwell to work, tell your line manager by phone or Teams message before 9:30 on the first day of absence. A text or email to a colleague is not enough.

Record each sickness absence in the HR Portal under **My Leave > Sickness** when you return to work.

## Fit notes

For absences of up to seven calendar days you can self-certify in the HR Portal. For absences longer than seven calendar days you need a fit note from a doctor, uploaded to the HR Portal.

## Sick pay

Employees with more than six months' service receive full pay for up to 20 working days of sickness absence in any rolling 12-month period, then statutory sick pay.

## Return to work

After any absence your manager holds a short return-to-work conversation to check you are well enough to work and whether any adjustments would help.
