# Remote Working Policy

## Hybrid working pattern

Most roles at Example Corp follow a hybrid pattern: at least two days per week in the office and up to three days working remotely. Teams agree their office days with their manager.

## Working from abroad

You may work remotely from another country for up to 10 working days per year, with your manager's approval and a completed request in the HR Portal. Longer periods need approval from HR and Legal because of tax and visa rules.

## Equipment and expenses

Remote workers can claim up to 150 for home-office equipment once every three years through the expenses system. Company laptops must be used for all work, see the IT knowledge base for laptop requests.

## Security when working remotely

Always connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems, lock your screen when away from your device, and never work on confidential documents in public spaces.
